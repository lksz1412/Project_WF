L199: 【63†Image: AI Not Used†img.shields.io】
L200: 
L201: Fully supports Retail, Forever and all the classics
L202: 
L203: This addon will completely replace your chat frame with a new one, with modern features:
L204:   * Even with the fancy features, it is fast.
L205:   * Stylish themes (Dark, Blizzard, ElvUI and GW2)
L206:   * Edit-mode free chat box resizing/moving
L207:   * Configurable timestamps
L208:   * Space out messages, not lines.
L209:   * Persistent command/typed chat history and navigatable with arrow keys
L210:   * 2,500+ message history, persisting across sessions
L211:   * All chat settings are global, across your account.
L212:   * Shorten frequently seen message components
L213:   * Change the fonts and sizes
